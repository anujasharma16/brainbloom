Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 October 2026

Brainbloom is a learning app that teaches how to understand and use AI through games, puzzles and quizzes. This page explains, in plain language, what information the app uses and why.

What stays on your device

Playing as a guest needs no account. Your progress (lessons finished, points, streak, badges), language, light/dark choice and sound settings are saved only in your own browser or app on your device. Clearing your browser data removes them.

If you create an account

  • Email address and an optional display name, used to sign you in and to send account emails (email verification and password reset).
  • Your password is handled by Google Firebase Authentication. We never see it or store it ourselves.
  • Your learning progress and language, saved to your account so it follows you to other devices.

We do not ask for payment details, your location, your contacts or any government ID.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your information.
  • We do not show advertising and do not use advertising trackers.
  • We do not record audio. The optional voice for Bruno and Pinky uses your device's built-in text-to-speech.

Services that help run Brainbloom

  • Google Firebase (Authentication and Firestore database) stores accounts and progress. See Google's privacy information at policies.google.com/privacy.
  • Website hosting providers receive technical details such as your IP address when you load the site, as any website does.
  • Code libraries (the 3D engine and Firebase) are loaded from public content networks (cdnjs and Google), which can see your IP address when your device downloads them.

Keeping and deleting your data

You can delete your account at any time in Profile → Delete my account. This removes your sign-in and your saved progress. Guest data can be removed by clearing your browser's site data.

Who the app is for

Brainbloom is intended for people aged 13 and over. If you are under the age of digital consent where you live, please use it together with a parent or guardian.

Educational content

The lessons, including the AI in Trading path, are for learning only. They are not financial, legal or professional advice, and trading can lose money.
